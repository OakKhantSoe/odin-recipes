Red Dragon Tail from Delicious in Dungeon (2024)

Description

Ready to feast like an adventurer? This Red Dragon Tail Steak from Delicious in Dungeon is a hearty, flavorful dish featuring a juicy meat dish for a truly dungeon-worthy meal!

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Yeast
  • Salt
  • Veggies for toppings
  • Chicken
  • Seasonings

Steps

  1. Mix flour, yeast, salt and water in a bowl and knead it until the right consistency.
  2. Slice your favourite toppings while the dough rests for a bit.
  3. Shape the dough and stretch it out. Add your favourite toppings.
  4. Take a large chunk of your preferred meat and slightly sprinkle some flour on the top.
  5. Place the dough on top of the meat and place it in the oven.
  6. With the rest of the meat, cut medium-sized chunks and skin it.
  7. Place the piece in the soup.
  8. Additionally, cut another piece of meat into a block shape and sear both sides.
  9. Dip the cooked piece in some seasonings to have yourself a lavish Dragon Tale feast.
