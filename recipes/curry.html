Curry from One Piece (1999)

Description

Craving a hearty meal fit for a Marine? This Marine Curry from One Piece is a comforting dish of rich, savory curry served with tender chunks of meat and vegetables alongside fluffy white rice!

Ingredients

  • Meat of your choice
  • Salt
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Yoghurt
  • Garam Masala
  • Turmeric
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Carrots
  • Pork Fat
  • Flour
  • Japanese Curry Powder
  • Bouillon
  • Apple

Steps

  1. Cut up medium-sized chunks of your preferred meat. Add garlic, ginger, turmeric and yoghurt to it. Mix it well and let it marinate.
  2. Boil a couple of tomatoes and peel them. Meanwhile, prepare a few veggies of your choice.
  3. Next, dice up some onions and saute them well in a pan over a strong flame. Let them simmer on a low heat without burning them.
  4. In another pan, melt the pork fat and then add the flour to mix it well over low heat.
  5. Add some curry powder and fresh garam masala.
  6. Get a stock pot and add all the ingredients, along with the bouillon and the marinated meat
  7. Garnish the curry with grated apple for a subtle sweet punch. Serve it hot and fresh.
