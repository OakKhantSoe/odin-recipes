Ginger Meatballs from Jujutsu Kaisen (2020)

Description

Craving something hearty and comforting? This Ginger Meatball recipe from Jujutsu Kaisen is packed with savory flavors and tender meatballs. This can be served alongside fresh vegetables and mushrooms for a satisfying meal straight from the anime!

Ingredients

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Egg Yolks
  • Ginger Paste
  • Green Onions
  • Ground Chicken

Steps

  1. Take a bowl and mix ground chicken, finely chopped green onions, salt, pepper, and yolks.
  2. You can also use the egg whites if you feel too lazy to separate the yolks.
  3. Mix the entire thing well until you achieve a thick consistency.
  4. Use your hands or two spoons to shape the mixture into tiny balls.
  5. Toss them into a pan and stir them well until cooked.
  6. Serve them with your favourite soup or sauce.
